Security that starts with visibility

I'm 0x0128, a cybersecurity and AI consultant based in Madrid, working in security operations.

My day to day is triaging and investigating alerts, analysing EDR detections, building SIEM use cases and SOAR playbooks, and turning the results into KPIs and dashboards that technical teams and executives can act on. I also assess and evolve SOCs and benchmark security tools.

Other of my recent work is about securing AI environments: models, agents, the data that flows into inference, and the guardrails, AI gateways, proxies and CASB that sit in front of them, tested with automated red teaming.

I like to analyse, summarise and prioritise so incidents get resolved with judgement. I work best in a team and enjoy helping junior colleagues find their feet. I'm studying Computer Engineering at UNED and building my own projects in the open.
